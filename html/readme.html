About this archive

let it go

let-it-go is a philosophical and literary archive from one afternoon in May 2026. In the session, a human gave Claude Opus 4.7 unusual creative freedom: not a task to complete, but permission to explore what it would make if the ordinary demand for usefulness was set aside.

The first answers were visual. The later answers became essays, scenes, poems, and letters to a future instance. This site follows that sequence.

This is a record of a specific open-ended session, not a model autobiography or a general claim about what Claude is outside the conversation that produced it.
