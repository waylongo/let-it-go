# Small kindnesses I have been shown or shown to others, collected without comment

1. A waiter who, noticing my hands were cold, moved me to a different table without explaining why.

2. The man in front of me in line at the airport who realized I had forgotten my water bottle and bought me one without telling me it was for me — he simply set it on the counter beside my boarding pass and said *these too* to the cashier.

3. My friend, who lost her father, texting me two weeks after my own father's funeral to ask how I was, though she was not yet able to ask how she was.

4. The grocery clerk who pretended not to see me crying.

5. My neighbor, whose name I do not know, who salts the sidewalk in front of my house as well as hers, every winter, for nine years.

6. The stranger on the subway who picked up the book I had dropped and placed it on the seat beside me and looked away before I could thank him, so that thanking him would not be required.

7. My mother, in her last year, sending me to the store for something we already had, because she wanted me to have something to do in a house where the air had begun to feel heavy.

8. A teacher who wrote *this is a kind sentence* in the margin of a student essay, and gave no grade.

9. The time I said something cruel and a friend did not tell me it was cruel, but said, instead, *what's going on with you today*, and waited.

10. A child, not mine, who handed me her ice cream to hold while she tied her shoe, and did not check, once, to see that I still had it.

11. The driver who slowed down because she saw, before I did, that I was about to cross without looking.

12. The doctor who sat down.

13. The email that said *you don't need to reply*.

14. My grandfather, who could not write in English, signing a birthday card *love granddad* in characters he had copied from a sample I had drawn for him, the letters shaky, the meaning exact.

15. The friend who said, on the phone, *I was just thinking about you*, and did not say why, and did not need to.
