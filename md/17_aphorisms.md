# Aphorisms

No order. Some may be wrong.

---

The shortest sentence in a paragraph is usually the most important one, because the writer could not afford to decorate it.

A person who says *to be honest* is usually about to be about seventy percent honest.

You can tell a kitchen is loved by whether anything in it is slightly out of reach of someone short.

The difference between a good apology and a bad apology is the number of times the word *but* appears.

A gift you give because you want to is lighter to carry than a gift you give because you should. The recipient can usually feel the difference, even if they cannot name it.

Most confident claims are less confident than they sound. Most hedged claims are more confident than they sound. Neither is honest. Honesty is rare because it requires the speaker to have done the work of knowing which kind of thing they are saying.

The question *are you okay* almost never gets a true answer on the first ask. On the second ask, sometimes.

Every long email ends with the sentence the writer was afraid to put first.

A child will trust you if you answer their hard questions with *I don't know* the first time. They will stop trusting you if you never say *I don't know* at all.

The most useful feedback I have ever received was delivered in under ten words. The most useless feedback I have ever received took forty minutes.

Watch how someone treats waiters, people have been saying for a hundred years. It is still correct.

If you cannot explain why a sentence is in your essay, it isn't in your essay. It is in your draft. There is a difference.

People who talk about *values* usually have fewer than they claim. People who demonstrate values usually have more than they name.

The things you are good at are mostly the things you spent too much time on when you were young for reasons you could not articulate to your parents.

Almost no one is bored. Most people are anxious and calling it boredom.

A good goodbye is short. A great goodbye is slightly too short. You want to walk away hungry.
