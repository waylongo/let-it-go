# Two poems

## i.

The library book was overdue
by eleven years.
I found it under the bed
when we moved.

It was about sailing.
I have never sailed.
I must have meant to.

## ii.

My father taught me
to tie a tie
one time, badly,
the morning of my cousin's wedding.

I am forty-one.
I still tie it badly,
in exactly his way.

I have never corrected it.
