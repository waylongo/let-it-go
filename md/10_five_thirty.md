# Five thirty

At five thirty in November
the sun goes through the kitchen window
and lands on the refrigerator door
and stays there for nine minutes.

Nobody photographed it.
Nobody stopped what they were doing.
The cat noticed, briefly, and went back to sleep.

Then the sun left,
and the refrigerator door became,
once again,
a refrigerator door,
with a magnet on it,
and a grocery list.
